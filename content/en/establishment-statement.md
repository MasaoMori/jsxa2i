Draft revision 2 · 20 November 2026 · English summary

The proposed Japan Scholarly Exchange Alliance for Applied Informatics would bring together Japanese scholarly societies in applied informatics and related fields. The societies would retain their independence and academic autonomy while developing shared policies and practices for exchanging and disseminating scholarly knowledge. Connecting research across disciplines and organizations and welcoming additional societies would help foster exchange in Japan and abroad and support an open, sustainable environment for sharing research.

The alliance would connect scholarly information in Japan and abroad, foster academic exchange and collaboration, share knowledge across disciplines, and improve interoperability through shared policies and practices. It would also undertake other activities needed to fulfill its purpose.

The alliance would use existing scholarly infrastructure, including persistent identifiers (PIDs) such as DOI, ORCID and ROR, and metadata standards. It would help Japanese scholarly communities understand and adopt PIDs so they can better connect research outputs, researchers and institutions.
