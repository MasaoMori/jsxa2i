Draft revision 2 · 20 November 2026 · English summary

The proposed Japan Scholarly Exchange Alliance for Applied Informatics would bring together independent Japanese scholarly societies. A shared approach to dissemination would make research and practical expertise easier to find, cite and reuse across disciplines and languages.

The alliance would connect scholarly information in Japan and abroad, support academic exchange and collaboration, share knowledge across disciplines, and improve interoperability. It would use existing scholarly infrastructure, including persistent identifiers such as DOI, ORCID and ROR, and help Japanese scholarly communities understand and adopt them. Common metadata practices would help link research outputs, researchers and institutions.
