## Following knowledge to its source

- Check authorship, publisher, publication year and bibliographic details; use persistent links such as DOIs where supplied.
- Distinguish summaries and introductions from original research and its review status.
- When translations are added, the proposed approach is to identify the source language, translation status and verification status.
- Reuse of full texts, figures and data follows the applicable license and permissions of authors and publishers.

## Scope of this draft

This draft has no independent paper database or populated catalogue. Follow the official entry points above to publications and activity information provided by each organization. Linked information is maintained by its original provider.
