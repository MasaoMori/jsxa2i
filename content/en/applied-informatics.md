## A working definition for this concept

Applied informatics is understood here as an interdisciplinary field that studies the collection, representation, analysis, circulation and use of information in diverse domains, connecting that inquiry to the understanding and resolution of real-world problems. Alongside technology development, it includes the meaning of information, its context of use and its effects on organizations and society.

This is a working definition for the AIKX concept. It does not represent a universal definition or an official position of either of the two organizations.

## Connecting areas of inquiry

| Area | Examples of shared questions |
| --- | --- |
| Institutional research | How can institutional data inform educational improvement and decision-making? |
| Educational and learning systems | How can information environments support learning and understanding of learning processes? |
| Data science and artificial intelligence | How can analysis and knowledge representation be used with attention to context and responsibility? |

These examples illustrate possible connections; they do not assign exclusive domains or roles to organizations.

## Connecting research and practice

By connecting research questions, theories and methods, application contexts and data, outputs and their implications, the concept seeks to make knowledge easier to reference, examine and use across fields.
