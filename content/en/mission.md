## Vision

To create an international scholarly environment in which knowledge in applied informatics circulates freely across nations, languages, disciplines, and the boundaries between research and practice, contributing to new knowledge and society.

## Mission

The proposed network aims to promote the international circulation of scholarly information in applied informatics and related fields.

Through cooperation among academic societies and research institutions, it seeks to support the collection, organization, sharing and dissemination of research outputs, scholarly insights and information about research activities. It also aims to encourage exchange between researchers and practitioners and the mutual use of knowledge across disciplines.

Through these activities, the concept seeks to advance applied informatics and the use of informatics knowledge in education, research, institutional management and society.

## A shared point of entry

The concept respects each organization's academic autonomy and existing publishing and communication services, connecting pathways to their knowledge. The AIKX name, organizational form, participating bodies and operator remain undecided. This page is a proposed statement, not an agreed founding charter.
