## Mission

The proposed alliance brings together Japanese scholarly societies in applied informatics and related fields to agree on how they share and disseminate scholarly knowledge. By connecting research and practical expertise across institutions and disciplines, it aims to support academic exchange in Japan and abroad and foster new research.

## How the alliance would work

The draft bylaws describe an unincorporated nonprofit alliance. Member societies retain their independence and academic autonomy and have equal voting rights. The alliance would welcome other eligible Japanese scholarly societies and work with scholarly organizations and research institutions abroad.
