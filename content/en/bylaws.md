Draft revision 6 · 20 November 2026 · English summary

The bylaws propose an unincorporated nonprofit alliance. Each member society retains its academic autonomy and has one representative and one vote on the Steering Council. Membership is open to eligible scholarly societies based in Japan. Organizations abroad may take part as collaborators.

Article 4 sets out six principles. Its third principle supports appropriate access and reuse, and calls for using international persistent identifiers (PIDs) for scholarly information and metadata standards to improve discovery, citation and interoperability.

The supplementary provisions name JSiSE and JAIR as the proposed founding societies. The bylaws would take effect on the date the societies agree upon following their respective approvals.
